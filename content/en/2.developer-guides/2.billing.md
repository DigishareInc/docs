---
title: Billing
description: How each message is charged to your Digishare balance, channel by channel.
icon: i-mdi-cash-multiple
---

Every message is paid from your company balance **before** it is sent. If the provider then refuses it, the amount is **refunded automatically** (you see a "Refund" line next to the charge in the app's **Usage Analysis** page).

::note
Prices come from your company's rate card, in your billing currency (MAD by default). Ask your Digishare contact for your rates.
::

## By channel

| Channel | What is charged | Price depends on |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WhatsApp** | Each **template** message, by its category: `marketing`, `utility`, `authentication`. A free-form message (no template) inside an open 24-hour conversation is charged at the `service` price. | The **recipient's country** (then its region, then your default price) |
| **SMS** | Each **segment** (160 GSM characters, or 70 with an emoji or another non-GSM character) | Your SMS rate |
| **Telegram** | **Each message** (campaigns, bot replies and agent replies), since 30 October 2026. Before that date Telegram messages were free | **0.02 MAD per message** by default (0.002 USD for USD accounts) |

### WhatsApp: when a template is free

- Inside a **free entry point** window (the customer came from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook page button), every template is free.
- Since 1 October 2026, a `utility` template sent inside an open 24-hour conversation is **charged** (it used to be free), as Meta bills it.

### WhatsApp: authentication-international price

Since 30 October 2026, an **authentication** template (OTP) sent to a recipient in one of these countries, **from a WhatsApp number of another country**, is charged at Meta's **authentication-international** price instead of the normal authentication price:

Bangladesh, Egypt, India, Indonesia, Iraq, Kuwait, Malaysia, Nepal, Nigeria, Oman, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Ukraine, United Arab Emirates.

Example: an OTP from a Moroccan number (+212) to a number in India (+91) uses the international price; the same OTP to a Moroccan number keeps the normal authentication price.

## When the balance is not enough

The message is not sent and nothing is charged. The API answers `402 Payment Required` with `Digishare balance not enough` (with the [async API](/developer-guides/campaigns/async-api), the event is rejected in the background instead). This now applies to **Telegram** as well.

A balance can be global (every channel) or restricted to one channel (for example `sms` or `telegram`). Make sure the channel you send on has an active credit, or that you have a global one.
