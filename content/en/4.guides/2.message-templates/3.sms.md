An SMS template is **plain text**: no image, no button, no formatting. There is **no review**.

## Steps

::steps

### Choose "SMS"

Open the editor (the **+** button at the top, then **Message template**) and choose **SMS**. Or, in an open template, click **Add SMS**.

### Write the text

Add variables with **Add variable**, or type `{{first_name}}`.

### Watch the counter

Under the text, the editor shows the **number of SMS parts**, the encoding (**GSM** or **Unicode**) and the characters left in the part.

### Save

Click **Create template**.

::

## How many parts?

A long SMS is sent in several **parts**. Each part counts.

| Encoding | 1 part | Per part, if the text is longer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GSM** (plain letters, digits) | 160 characters | 153 characters |
| **Unicode** (emojis, Arabic, some accents) | 70 characters | 67 characters |

::warning
**One emoji or special character** turns the whole SMS into Unicode: 70 characters per part instead of 160. The editor warns you.
::

## Good to know

- Variables count with **their real value**. A long first name makes the SMS longer.
- Keep links **short**: they take many characters.
- The **same template** can hold an SMS text, a WhatsApp text and a Telegram text. The variables are shared.
