An e-mail template holds the **body** of the e-mail. There is **no review**. The e-mail **subject** is set in the campaign.

## Steps

::steps

### Choose "Email"

Open the editor (the **+** button at the top, then **Message template**) and choose **Email**. Or, in an open template, click **Add Email**.

### Choose the editor

- **Drag & drop**: a visual editor. Drag blocks (text, image, button, columns) from the left.
- **HTML**: paste or write your own HTML code.

### Add variables

Type `{{first_name}}` in the text.

In the **Drag & drop** editor, first click **Get variables**, then type `@` in a text to pick one of the template's variables.

### Save

Click **Create template**.

::

::warning
In the **Drag & drop** editor, your work is kept when **the mouse leaves the editor**. Before saving, move the mouse out of the editing area.
::

## Good to know

- Switching editors (**Drag & drop** ↔ **HTML**) **erases** the e-mail's current content. The editor asks you to confirm.
- The **same template** can hold an e-mail and a WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS text. The variables are shared.
